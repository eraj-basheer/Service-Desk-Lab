# KB-01 - How to register for multi-factor authentication

**Status:** ☐ Draft
**Audience:** all staff
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11

## What this fixes

You will be asked to set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) the first time you sign in, and again whenever your security methods are reset. This article walks you through registering the Microsoft Authenticator app.

## Before you start

- Your phone, with the **Microsoft Authenticator** app installed (App Store or Google Play)
- Your temporary or current password
- A second method available, such as a phone number or another email address

## Steps

1. Go to `portal.office.com` and sign in with your work email and password.
2. When prompted, choose **Next** to start the security setup.
3. Select **Authenticator app** and choose **Next**.
4. Open Microsoft Authenticator on your phone, tap **+**, and choose **Work or school account**.
5. Point your phone camera at the QR code on the screen.
6. Approve the test notification that appears on your phone.
7. Register a backup method - a phone number or a second email address - when prompted.
8. Choose **Done**.

You will only be asked to approve a sign-in when you use a new device or browser, or when your session expires.

## If it still does not work

- **The QR code will not scan:** choose **I can't scan the QR code** and enter the code shown on screen instead.
- **No notification arrives:** check that notifications are enabled for the Authenticator app, and that your phone has internet access.
- **You have a new phone:** the service desk must reset your methods before you can register again - see KB-04.
- **Still stuck:** contact the service desk with the exact error message and the time it appeared.
