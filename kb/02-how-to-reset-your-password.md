# KB-02 - How to reset your own password

**Status:** ☐ Draft
**Audience:** all staff
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11

## What this fixes

You have forgotten your password, or it has expired, and you want to reset it without waiting for the service desk.

## Before you start

- Your registered MFA method - the Authenticator app or your registered phone number
- A device with internet access

## Steps

1. Go to `aka.ms/sspr`.
2. Enter your work email address and the characters shown in the image, then choose **Next**.
3. Choose **I forgot my password**.
4. Verify your identity using your registered method:
   - Approve the notification in Microsoft Authenticator, or
   - Enter the code sent to your phone or backup email.
5. Enter a new password twice. It must meet the organisation's complexity rules.
6. Choose **Finish**, then sign in with the new password.

## Password rules

- At least 12 characters
- A mix of upper and lower case, numbers and symbols
- Not one of your previous passwords
- Not your name, your email address, or a single dictionary word

## If it still does not work

- **You are not registered for self-service reset:** the service desk must enable it for you.
- **You no longer have access to your registered method:** the service desk must verify your identity and reset your methods. Bring photo ID.
- **The new password is rejected:** check the complexity rules above. Avoid reusing a previous password.
- **Locked out entirely:** see KB-04.
