# KB-04 - What to do when you are locked out

**Status:** ☐ Draft
**Audience:** all staff
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11

## What this fixes

Too many failed sign-in attempts have locked your account, or you cannot complete multi-factor authentication, so you cannot get into Microsoft 365.

## Before you start

- Your phone, if your MFA method still works
- Your employee or student ID, if you need to contact the service desk

## Try self-service first

1. Go to `aka.ms/sspr`.
2. Enter your work email and complete the verification.
3. Choose **I forgot my password** or **My account is locked**.
4. Verify with your registered method.
5. Set a new password and sign in.

Wait a few minutes before retrying. Repeated attempts extend the lockout.

## If you cannot use self-service

Contact the service desk. You will be asked to verify your identity before anything is changed - usually photo ID, employee number, or a call-back to your manager.

Have this ready:

- Your full name and username
- The time the problem started
- The exact error message
- Whether your MFA method is still available

## What the service desk will do

1. Confirm your identity.
2. Reset the password or unlock the account.
3. Reset MFA methods if your device is lost or replaced.
4. Ask you to register again on first sign-in.

## If your phone is lost or stolen

Tell the service desk immediately. Your MFA methods will be reset so the old device cannot be used to approve sign-ins.

## If it still does not work

- **You are locked out again straight away:** something may be repeatedly trying your old password - a mail client on a phone, or a saved credential. The service desk can check the sign-in logs.
- **Self-service says you are not registered:** the service desk must enable it.
